--- RECOVERY CORP INTERNAL POLICY ---

TOPIC 1: LEAVE AND TIME OFF
All full-time employees receive 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Vacation requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least two weeks in advance. Unused leave can carry over to the next year, up to a maximum of 5 business days.

TOPIC 2: SHIPPING AND LOGISTICS
Standard physical shipments to domestic clients are dispatched every Tuesday and Thursday at 3:00 PM. Any order placed after 1:00 PM on those days will be processed on the following shipping day. International shipments require a signed customs clearance form prior to warehouse release.

TOPIC 3: REFUND POLICY
Customers are eligible for a full monetary refund on software licenses within 14 days of purchase, provided the product key has not been activated. Hardware returns are accepted within 30 days but are subject to a 10% restocking fee if the factory seal is broken.
